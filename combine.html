How the EAs fit together

Running several Expert Advisors on one account only helps when they do not all trade the same way at the same time. This page lays out how each of our systems trades and which pairs are built differently from each other.

How the pairs are rated

Each system is described by three things that come from its own design: the market it trades, what starts a trade, and how long a trade usually lasts.

  • Different market. The two systems trade different instruments, so they do not share a price path. This is the strongest form of difference.
  • Complements. Same market, but both what starts a trade and how long it lasts differ.
  • Partly overlaps. Same market, and one of the two is the same.
  • Overlaps. Same market, same kind of trigger and same holding style. They will often be in a trade at the same moment and share the same bad days.

What starts a trade falls into five groups: Break (a pending order beyond a level or range, entering with the break), Reaction (trading back from a stretch or against a break), Trend (entering a dip inside an established trend), Momentum (an oscillator extreme inside a session) and Session (a roster of hour-by-hour specialists). How long a trade lasts falls into four groups: seconds to minutes (Scalp), Hours, Days, and a Session that is always flat by the close.

What this matrix is, and is not

It is built from how each system is designed to trade. It is not a measured profit correlation, and it does not promise that a pair behaves as rated. Systems on the same gold chart still share the same market, the same spread and the same margin, so a combined account needs its own test and its own risk limits. Reading it

  • Most gold systems are breakout systems, so the gold-to-gold cells are mostly Overlaps or Partly overlaps. Adding a second breakout system on gold adds trades that tend to arrive together.
  • The clearest differences are across markets: the Bitcoin, NASDAQ 100 and AUDCAD systems trade other instruments entirely.
  • On gold, the Reaction systems (a short punch at a level, a snap back, a fade of a break) are built differently from the Break systems, and The Crew works by hour of day, not by level.
  • A guard such as Nanny does not trade at all, so it is not in the matrix; it watches the whole account.
