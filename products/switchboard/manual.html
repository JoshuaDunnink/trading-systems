Switchboard - user manual

What it does

Switchboard runs nine separate "lines" on gold (XAUUSD). Each line watches recent swing highs and lows on its own timeframe and leaves one buy stop just below a swing high and one sell stop just above a swing low. When price breaks through, the order fills.

Every trade has: - a stop loss of at most 10.00 (price units), set the moment the order is placed; - a take profit; - a lock: once the trade has moved far enough in your favour, the stop moves into profit; - a trailing stop on lines 4, 5, 7 and 9; - an early close on lines 5-9: if the first candle after the fill closes against the trade, it is closed at once.

A line keeps at most one resting order per direction. Positions from earlier fills keep running, each with its own stop, so several can be open at once (up to 35 at the busiest moment in the four-year BlackBull test). Switchboard never adds to a losing trade and never raises the lot after a loss.

The control panel

Pause (resting orders are removed, open trades keep their exits) / Resume, Refresh orders, Lock profit (moves the stop of the selected trade to its line's lock level, or to break-even), Close all. The status line says why Switchboard is or is not trading.

How much money it needs

Lots grow with the balance (Risk level Medium: per line about balance x 8-33 / gold price / 1000). Below about 5,000 the formula asks for less than the broker's minimum of 0.01 lot, so every line trades 0.01 - more risk than designed. With every line at 0.01 lot the worst losing stretch in the tests was 745 (BlackBull, 26 Aug - 17 Oct 2024):

Practical notes

  • Attach to one XAUUSD chart (any timeframe). One copy per account unless the magic numbers differ.
  • Start on Low. Run it on its own account: it tends to gain and dip in the same weeks as other gold breakout EAs.
  • Keep the terminal running; after a restart Switchboard reads its open trades and continues.
