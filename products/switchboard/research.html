Switchboard - Research Note

What the machine does, how each part is bounded, and what the tests behind each figure are.

Version 1.02 - October 2026

Every figure in this note is a Strategy Tester run on real broker ticks; those are backtests, not a record of live trading. Every number names the broker feed and the window it came from.

0. The result in one table

Backtest, MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, real ticks, version 1.02 at its defaults (Risk level Medium, lots growing with the balance), deposit 10,000, 150 ms execution delay.

feed trades profit factor relative equity drawdown net
BlackBull, 2022-11-01 to 2026-10-12 (headline) 4,880 2.03 24.49 % +62,341
Vantage, 2025-07-04 to 2026-10-12 1,154 3.23 9.19 % +14,040

The same windows at Risk level Low: BlackBull +17,082, profit factor 1.89, drawdown 12.55 %; Vantage +5,556, profit factor 3.10, drawdown 4.47 %.

Backtest, default settings, BlackBull, 2022-11-01 to 2026-10-12: the lot grows with the balance, so most of the money in that run was made late in the window on larger lots, and the deepest drawdown (September to November 2024) came when the account had already grown to about 28,000. With every line at a fixed 0.01 lot the same stretch cost 745, which is 7.5 % of a 10,000 account.

1. Which market the history belongs to

Switchboard earns most when gold moves a lot. Backtest, default settings, BlackBull, 2022-11-01 to 2026-10-12: the account was flat for most of the quiet 2023 market, climbed through 2024, gave back about a fifth of its balance in autumn 2024, and rose steadily through 2025 and 2026. Backtest, default settings, 13 October 2025 to 12 October 2026: the default settings made a profit factor of 3.16 on BlackBull and 3.83 on VT, with drawdowns of 4.33 % and 3.21 %. Quiet weeks slow it down; they are part of the record above.

2. How the machine is built

Nine lines, each with its own timeframe (three daily, one four-hour, five hourly). A line looks for the nearest swing high above price and the nearest swing low below price that is confirmed (a fixed number of bars old) and that no later bar has traded through. It rests a buy stop a small fixed share of price below the swing high and a sell stop the same way above the swing low, at most one resting order per direction. Orders expire after the line's life (15 hours to about 20 days).

Every position has a stop loss (at most 10.00 price units) and a take profit from the start. Once it has moved a set share of price in its favour the stop moves into profit; on four lines it then trails. On the five hourly lines a position whose first one-, five- or fifteen-minute candle closes against it is closed at once. Positions from earlier fills keep running beside new ones; the busiest moment of the BlackBull run held 35 open positions, each with its own stop.

3. How much money it needs

Below about 5,000 the lot formula asks for less than the broker's minimum lot, so every line trades 0.01 and the account carries more risk than designed. At 0.01 per line the worst losing stretch in the tests (745) is 37 % of a 2,000 account, 25 % of 3,000 and 15 % of 5,000.

4. What was tested and left out

  • Market-state gates (how busy gold has been, trend versus chop, range expansion, each ranked against its own past year): no state was a losing one across the eras tested, so a gate would only have removed profitable trades.
  • Sizing up in busy markets: improved two runs and worsened the drawdown on two others; not adopted.
  • Fourteen alternative exits (earlier or later locks, a closer trail, partial closes): none beat the shipped exits in the current market.
  • A recovery grid: after a losing trade, price kept going against it more often than chance in the current market; averaging in would have added size into that move. Not used.

5. Protections

Stop loss on every order; equity stop (setting, default 30 % below the equity peak: everything is closed and no new orders are placed that day); US jobs-report pause; maximum spread; broker clock check; the control panel (pause, refresh, lock profit, close all). After a terminal restart the EA re-reads its trades; locked stops stay locked.
