The Operator sits at a switchboard with nine lines. Each line listens to gold at its own pace - three to the daily chart, one to the four-hour chart, five to the hourly chart - and she only connects a call when price actually breaks through the level a line is watching. A call that goes quiet the moment it is connected is hung up at once.

Switchboard is an Expert Advisor for MetaTrader 5 (MT5) that trades gold (XAUUSD) with nine independent lines on one chart.

How a trade is made:

Each line finds the nearest swing high above price and the nearest swing low below price that is confirmed (a fixed number of bars old) and that no later bar has traded through. It rests one buy stop a small, fixed share of price below that swing high, and one sell stop the same way above the swing low. A line keeps at most one resting order per direction; positions from earlier fills keep running, each with its own stop. Orders that are not triggered expire after the line's own life, from 15 hours on the fastest line to about 20 days on the slowest.

How a trade is protected:

  • Every order carries a stop loss of at most 10.00 (price units) and a take profit from the moment it is placed.
  • Once the trade has moved a set share of price in its favour, the stop moves into profit.
  • On four lines the stop then trails behind price, minute by minute.
  • On the five hourly lines, a trade whose first candle (one, five or fifteen minutes) closes against it is closed at once.
  • On US jobs-report days no new orders rest between 13:50 and 16:30 server time.
  • An equity stop (default 30 % below the equity peak) closes everything and pauses the rest of the day.

What it does not do: no grid, no averaging into a losing trade, no martingale, no hedging of a loss.

Settings start at the top: Risk level (Low, Medium, High, or a custom multiplier), a virtual balance, a maximum spread in USD, then nine line switches, the jobs-report pause, the equity stop and the broker clock. The control panel pauses and resumes the EA, refreshes orders, locks profit on a selected trade and closes everything.

Backtest results, MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, real ticks, default settings: see the Research Note. The EA earns most when gold moves a lot and slows down in quiet markets.

FAQ

Which broker? Any broker offering XAUUSD on a hedging account. Raw-spread accounts were used for testing.

Does it use a grid or martingale? No. Every position has its own stop loss; a line keeps at most one resting order per direction, and the lot never grows after a loss. Several positions can be open at once when several lines (or later breaks on one line) fill - in the four-year BlackBull test up to 35 at the busiest moment, each with its own stop.

Can I run it beside other EAs? Yes, it uses its own magic number. It tends to gain and dip in the same weeks as other gold breakout EAs, so a separate account keeps the drawdowns apart.

What happens after a restart? It reads its open trades and orders and continues; locked stops stay locked.
