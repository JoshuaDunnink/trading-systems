The busy one. A channel breakout, several swing breakouts and a row of small scalpers, each with its own stop.

What it does. Gold breakout Expert Advisor with four parts on one account. A channel breakout rests stop orders just beyond the highest high and lowest low of a long channel on the 15-minute chart, with a stop, a target and a break-even lock. Three swing-breakout legs rest a stop order just beyond confirmed swing highs and lows, lock a small profit and trail it. A row of fixed-dollar scalpers rests stop orders just inside the hourly and 15-minute swing highs and lows, each with a stop of a few dollars and a tight trailing stop. Every position has a stop loss from the moment it opens. No martingale, no grid, no averaging.

Instrument and chart. Gold (XAUUSD, any broker suffix). Tested on the M15 chart. The orders are placed by price level, so the chart period does not change the trades.

How it behaves. Swift is the busiest of the three Gold EAs, with about thirty trades a week, most of them closed within minutes to hours; some positions stay open for days. In a busy gold market it earns in many small steps. In older, calmer markets replayed at today's gold price the research tests lost most of a $100 account in some periods, so it has little room when gold turns calm. A few large winners make a share of the profit: the research note shows the same run without its biggest winners.

Settings. One setting matters: the lot size per order, 0.01 by default. The research inputs are not meant to be changed.

Please note. Results shown in the research note are strategy-tester results with stated limits. A forward test on a demo account began on 8 November 2026; there is no live record yet. Run it on a demo account first.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Do I need a .set file?

A: No. The defaults are the tested settings. Leave the other inputs alone.

Q: What is the minimum balance?

A: The manual explains it. In short: 0.01 lot needs about $9 of margin at 1:500 and about $150 at 1:30 with gold near $4,500, and the largest single loss in the research run was about $23 at that size, so a small account has little room.

Q: Which chart does it need?

A: A gold chart (XAUUSD). It was tested on M15.

Q: Does it use martingale or a grid?

A: No. Every position has its own stop loss, and the lot size is fixed.
