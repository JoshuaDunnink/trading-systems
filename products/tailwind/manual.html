Tailwind - manual

1. Install and start (five minutes)

  1. Copy TAILWIND.ex5 into MQL5\Experts, restart the terminal or refresh the Navigator.
  2. Open a Bitcoin (BTCUSD) chart on H1. The EA refuses any other instrument.
  3. Drag the EA onto the chart, tick Allow Algo Trading, keep the defaults.
  4. Choose your Risk level (Low / Medium / High) and press OK. That is the only setting you need.
  5. Run it on a demo account first: the tester results are not a forecast and the live fill path has not been tested.

2. Settings: start at the top

  • Risk level: Low = half the validated size, Medium = the validated size, High = one and a half times. All other settings are fixed at the validated values.
  • Allow longs / Allow shorts: switch a side off if you want to trade one direction only.

3. What it does, in order

Trend pullback re-entry on bitcoin. The trend is a fast average above a slow one with the slow one rising; the entry is a short, deep dip in the momentum index that turns back up. The stop starts just beyond the dip and then trails at a multiple of the average range.

4. How much money do I need? (minimum balance)

Recommended minimum: $2,000. Below it the broker's minimum lot (0.01) becomes the real risk instead of the setting. Higher risk levels need a larger balance for the same safety.

5. When it will not trade

  • On the wrong instrument (it refuses the chart).
  • When the market is closed, when the spread or broker stop level blocks the order (it logs the reason in the Experts tab).

6. Known limits

  • About one trade in four wins: the profit comes from a few long trend rides. Without the largest 5 % of trades the result in this window is negative.
  • A trend system: it gives back open profit in sideways markets and needs patience through losing runs.
  • Bitcoin trades all week, including weekends; stops can slip in fast moves.
  • Backtest results only: no forward or live record yet.
  • Past results do not predict future results.

7. Frequently asked

Do I need a .set file? No. The defaults are the validated settings. Can I change the engine? No: the engine values are fixed on purpose. Only the risk level and (where shown) the directions are yours. Does it use martingale, grid or averaging? No. One position at a time, with a stop on every trade.
