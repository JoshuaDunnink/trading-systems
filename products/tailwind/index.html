He waits for a dip inside a strong bitcoin uptrend (or a pop inside a downtrend), steps in as the move resumes, and stays in while the trend lasts.

What it does. Trend pullback re-entry on bitcoin. The trend is a fast average above a slow one with the slow one rising; the entry is a short, deep dip in the momentum index that turns back up. The stop starts just beyond the dip and then trails at six and a half times the average range.

Instrument and chart. Bitcoin (BTCUSD), H1 chart. Holds a trade about one day (median) and up to several days, stop trails behind price at six and a half average ranges, no fixed target.

Settings. Tailwind has one main input, the risk level. It runs one position at a time with a stop on every trade, without martingale, grid or averaging.

Please note. Results shown in the research note are strategy-tester results with stated limits. Run on a demo account first.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Do I need a .set file?

A: No. The defaults are the validated settings; you choose a risk level (Low, Medium or High).

Q: What is the minimum balance?

A: About $2,000. Below that the broker's minimum lot becomes the risk instead of the setting.

Q: Which chart does it need?

A: A Bitcoin (BTCUSD) chart on H1. It refuses other instruments.

Q: Does it use martingale or a grid?

A: No. One position at a time, with a stop on every trade.
