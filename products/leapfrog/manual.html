Leapfrog — User Manual

Instrument: XAUUSD (gold) · Timeframe: any (it works from hourly and daily series internally; M15 is used for validation) Magic: 89770 (the five ladders use 89770–89774) · Version: 1.30 Account type: Hedging

What it does

Five independent "ladders" each watch a different rolling range of gold: the last day (ladder D), about 30 hours (E), about 56 hours (A), about three days (C) and four trading weeks (B). Each ladder rests a stop order at the edge of its range so that a genuine break of that range fills it in the direction of the break. Every order carries a hard stop from the moment it is placed, set as a percentage of the gold price (about 0.3 % for A, C, D and E, about 1 % for B). Once a position is in profit, the exit engine closes half of it at 0.71 R, half of the rest at 0.89 R and half again at 1.01 R (R = the position's own stop distance), and trails the stop 0.71 R behind the best price. There is no averaging down, no grid, no martingale: each ladder holds at most one position per side, and a losing position is closed at its stop.

The ranges often end at the same high or low, so on a clean break several ladders fill at once: most breaks are traded by two to four ladders together. Treat the five ladders as one strategy traded at up to five times the lot per ladder, not as five unrelated systems (chapter "Deposit and position sizing").

What changed in version 1.30

  • Trades identical to the previous version (proved deal for deal). It no longer sends a new stop order into a closed market (the broker's daily break, a holiday): the request the broker would have refused is skipped, so the trades are unchanged and the journal no longer fills with 'Market closed' errors.

What changed in version 1.29 - panel, chart labels, restart-safe

  • Trades identical to 1.28 (proved deal for deal; see the MANIFEST).
  • Faster backtests and less VPS load. Unchanged in practice - Leapfrog was already fast (about 2 minutes for 269 M BlackBull ticks); its per-tick work fell further (the daily ATR read once a day).
  • A restart no longer re-places an order on a level that already traded. Each ladder's last levels, its side, its cooldown after a stop, and the hour of its last order check are saved on the account and re-read.
  • No refused fills on a small account. A new stop order is placed only when the free margin covers it; when it does not, the panel and a daily journal line say so. At the tested sizes this never binds.
  • Clearer journal. The start lines say when the account type's size overrides your lot inputs, and when edited [Custom] inputs are not in force under Settings = Recommended.
  • A control panel and chart labels (below).

What is new in version 1.21 — two volatility-regime controls

Version 1.21 had one weakness, and it was documented honestly: the engine earns when gold's daily ranges expand and pays a slow cost while they do not. Version 1.21 addresses that directly, with two controls that read the market's own volatility against its own recent history. Both are on by default.

1. Daily volatility stand-down. The engine measures the previous day's average true range as a percentile of its own last 250 days, and does not arm new orders when that percentile is in the top 2% or the bottom 10%. A range-breakout system is run over when volatility explodes and noise-baited when it collapses; it earns in the middle of the distribution. Measured over eight years, the days in the top 2% of daily volatility cost more than every other day combined.

2. Hourly channel-width position sizing. The width of the hourly 20-bar channel, again as a percentile of its own recent history, scales the position size — smaller when the channel is narrow, larger when it is wide, on a curve rather than a switch. Nothing is skipped: a narrow-channel trade is taken at reduced size rather than discarded, because those trades still earn in a trending market even though they earn less. The tenth ladder-like exception is ladder B, whose ten-day range measures a different thing entirely; it is exempt from the sizing and trades at full size.

Both controls are self-calibrating. They compare gold to its own recent behaviour, so they need no retuning when gold's price level or typical range changes.

Getting started

  1. Attach to an XAUUSD (gold) chart. No set file is needed; the defaults are the validated ones.
  2. Confirm the journal banner reads Leapfrog v1.28 | settings Recommended | account Personal | Symbol=XAUUSD | ... and the next line Leapfrog sizing: base lot per ladder ... | spread limit off.
  3. Leave it running on a VPS. It maintains resting orders around the clock and manages stops on every tick.

What changed in version 1.21

Backtest, MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, real ticks, OANDA GOLD.pro, 2018-07-01 to 2026-09-07 (the 8.2-year full-cycle window).

Version 1.21 shipped a day-expansion filter. Its input read "only arm when the day is expanding", but the code did something else: it let the order fill and then closed the position, paying a full spread on every rejection. It fired on 24-40% of all fills and it cut winners — across the full cycle the position-level win rate is 50.4% with it and 68.1% without.

Switching it off takes the eight-year record from +10,317 to +20,388 and reduces equity drawdown from 15.1% to 9.9%. Better return at lower risk, on all five brokers.

Rebuilding the filter properly — checking the condition before placing the order, which is what the label always promised — was tested and is better than the old behaviour but still worse than removing it. Both forms remain available (Expansion gate: 0=off 1=after fill 2=on arm) and both ship off.

What changed in version 1.22

Version 1.22 adds one optional input and changes nothing else. Size by the last closed event's outcome (off by default) scales each new order up after the engine's last completed break won and down after it lost, by the percentage in Tilt. It was built because an earlier version of the engine showed its profits arriving in runs. On the current engine that pattern is gone: the two volatility controls of version 1.21 already remove the days that produced the runs, and with the option switched on the engine earned 4–15 % less over fourteen months of real broker ticks on three brokers. It therefore ships off. With the option off, version 1.22 places exactly the same orders as version 1.21.

  • 1.28 — one screen of settings (chapter "Settings: start at the top"). Prop-firm rules: your firm's own numbers (since 1.26)

The Account type (chapter "Prop-firm accounts: one choice") chooses the size. The group right under it, Prop-firm rules, enforces your firm's limits. Choosing Prop firm 2-step or Prop firm 1-step already fills in the two loss limits for you (5 % daily and 10 % maximum, or 3 % and 6 %; both fixed, and the EA acts 0.5 % before each); type your firm's own numbers to override them. With Personal every rule is off until you type a number.

When a limit is about to be reached, the EA closes its own trades, deletes its own orders and stops opening new ones: until the firm's next day for the daily rules, for good for the maximum loss and the profit target. The Journal says which rule acted and at what equity:

LEAPFROG prop-firm rule: daily loss limit (equity 95512.40, day start 100020.00, firm limit 95020.00) - trades closed, orders deleted, paused until the firm's next day

and the start line lists what is on:

LEAPFROG prop-firm rules: account size 100000.00 | daily loss 5.00% of account size from the higher of day-start balance and equity - the EA acts at 4.50% of account size | maximum loss 10.00% (fixed) - the EA acts at 9.50% | ...

All six count the daily loss as a share of the account size from the previous day's closing balance, open trades included. Leave counted from at the higher of balance and equity - it is never later than the firm's own meter.

Profit target: set it for the challenge phases only. When the target is reached the EA stops for good on that login; on the funded account set it back to 0.

The firm's day. Most firms restart the daily loss at midnight of the platform's own clock: leave 0. If your firm names another time, convert it to the Market Watch clock. Gold is closed for about an hour around the broker's midnight on most platforms, so for gold an hour's difference rarely matters; a market that trades through midnight (Bitcoin) needs the exact hour.

Restarts. The EA remembers the account size, the day's starting value, the highest balance and whether a rule stopped it, across restarts of the platform (terminal global variables named PR<login>_<magic>_...). A restart in the middle of the day does not give it a fresh daily allowance.

What these rules do not do

  • They close this EA's trades only. The limits are measured on the whole account's equity, so trades from another EA or by hand count toward them; give every EA on the account the same numbers.
  • The EA acts on the first price tick past its limit. A gap or a very fast market can carry the loss a little further before the closing trades fill - that is what the safety margin is for; do not set it to 0 on a real challenge.
  • No news filter. Some funded accounts forbid opening or closing trades a few minutes around high-impact releases. The platform's economic calendar cannot be replayed in the Strategy Tester, so a news rule could not be proved the way every rule above was; pause the EA by hand around those releases if your firm requires it.
  • No minimum trade duration and no minimum number of trading days.

Proof. With every rule off, this version placed the same deals as the version before it, deal for deal (107 trades, net +309.21, Pepperstone real ticks, 28 August - 19 September 2026). Each rule was then switched on at a level the same weeks reach; at all 11 triggers the EA closed its trades, opened nothing until the pause ended, and placed exactly the same deals as with the rules off up to the first trigger.

Deposit and position sizing

The default sizes 0.04 lot per ladder per 10,000 USD of equity and rounds down to the broker's minimum lot. Below roughly 2,500 USD every ladder trades the 0.01 minimum, so the strategy behaves like a fixed-lot system and its drawdown becomes a larger share of the balance. The hourly sizing control is also blunted on small accounts: it cannot size below the broker minimum, so the reduction it applies in narrow-channel conditions is partly lost. Recommended minimum: 5,000 USD; 10,000 USD for the figures above to apply as printed.

Most breaks are traded by two to four ladders at the same second (measured: 81-84 % of positions sit in such events), so the exposure on one break is up to five times the lot per ladder. Switching ladders off lowers that exposure but costs more in the worst weeks than it saves over a full cycle (measured over 8 years: every two- or three-ladder subset had a 20-75 % deeper worst week for the same profit), which is why the minimum stays at 5,000 USD rather than 'run fewer ladders'.

Requirements and limits

  • Gold (XAUUSD or your broker's spelling). On another instrument it loads, prints that it will not trade, and stays flat.
  • Hedging account. The five ladders can hold positions on both sides at once.
  • A raw-spread or ECN account is preferred: the partial-profit marks are small fractions of the daily range, and a wide spread takes a visible share of them.
  • The volatility controls need history to calibrate: 250 daily bars and 200 hourly bars. On a fresh symbol with little history they return "unknown" and the engine trades ungated rather than standing down on missing data.
  • Backtested on real broker tick data. Backtest results only — not a promise or a forecast. Test on a demo account first.

Is it waiting, or is it broken? — the regime monitor

This product earns in a particular market state and goes quiet outside it. That is disclosed above, but a disclosure without a number is not usable: meeting a flat stretch, you cannot tell whether the machine is waiting for its conditions or whether its edge has changed. This section gives you the one measurement that separates those, taken from this product's own record.

The state to watch is the daily Average True Range of the traded instrument, expressed as a percentile of its own trailing 250 days — not a fixed number of dollars or pips, which goes stale as the instrument reprices. Most charting packages will plot ATR; the percentile is simply where today's ATR sits within the last year of its own values.

Measured in the Strategy Tester on 8.2 years of OANDA GOLD.pro real ticks, 2018 to 2026 (92 months). Monthly net by that month's volatility band:

band months mean per month
below 0.35 18 +$16
0.35 – 0.50 16 below breakeven
0.50 – 0.65 18 +$72
0.65 – 0.80 15 +$320
above 0.80 25 +$251

Backtest, MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, real ticks, OANDA GOLD.pro, 2018 to 2026.

These are MONTHS, not the EA's own gate — do not read them as a gap in it. The stand-down reads each day's percentile, and by that daily measure the only two losing bands over 8.2 years are the bottom 10% and the top 2% — which are exactly the two the EA already stands down in. Every band between them is positive or flat by day. Raising the lower bound to 0.35 or 0.50 was tested and costs net (+$3,347 shipped against +$3,019 and +$2,799).

Backtest, MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, real ticks, OANDA GOLD.pro, 2018 to 2026.

Why a month can lose while its days do not: inside those sub-0.50 months the damage is done by their more active days — days at the 50–65 percentile underperform inside a quiet month, while the same percentile earns $5 a day across the record as a whole. What hurts is volatility that is high against its own recent neighbourhood, not volatility that is low in absolute terms. An acceleration filter for exactly that was tested and rejected: it added cost without improving drawdown, so it is not part of the shipped engine.

Backtest, MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, real ticks, OANDA GOLD.pro, 2018 to 2026.

Good state: volatility percentile at or above 0.50 (63% of the record). In that state it earned in 60% of months, median +$80, and its deepest three-month dip stayed within about 10% of a 10,000 USD account — the tripwire below.

Tripwire — while volatility is at or above 0.50, a three-month dip deeper than about 10% of the account is outside anything the record contains. Below 0.50, a flat or mildly negative stretch is documented behaviour and is not evidence of decay.

Backtest, MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, real ticks, OANDA GOLD.pro, 2018 to 2026.

Read this alongside the concentration. The top 3 months of 99 carry 78% of all profit; the top 6 carry more than 100% of it, meaning the remaining 93 do not add further profit in aggregate. This engine waits and then earns in bursts. Any three-month window is close to uninformative about it, in either direction.

What this cannot tell you. While the market is outside the good state, a temporary quiet period and a permanent change in behaviour look identical. The tripwire is the earliest point at which they separate, not an early warning. Nothing here is a guarantee; it is the historical extreme of a backtested record, and being beyond it is evidence rather than proof.
