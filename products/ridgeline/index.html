Level breaks, nothing else: a gold H1 breakout system that trades only when a confirmed swing level actually breaks, with a hard stop on every trade.

What it does. Ridgeline trades the ridge: the swing high or low that price has to climb over. It places a pending stop order just beyond the latest confirmed daily swing level, so a position opens only when price actually breaks through. Every trade carries a hard stop from the fill, a target, and a trailing stop. Each level is traded once, and the order is removed if price moves away without breaking it.

Instrument and chart. Gold (XAUUSD, any suffix), H1 chart. The stop distance follows gold's own recent volatility (a fraction of its 14-day average daily range), so it widens and narrows with the market.

Settings. No settings file is needed. The first input group has four inputs: Recommended or Custom settings, a risk level (Low, Medium, High), a virtual balance and a maximum spread in USD. Everything else is ignored unless Settings is Custom.

Please note. One position at a time, a stop on every trade, no martingale, no grid, no averaging. Trades are few, roughly 30 to 70 a year, so results arrive slowly, and gold breakout systems earn mostly in trending phases. Results shown are strategy-tester results with stated limits. Frequently asked questions

Q: Do I need a .set file?

A: No. The defaults are the validated settings; you choose a risk level (Low, Medium or High).

Q: How often does it trade?

A: Rarely. Roughly 30 to 70 trades a year, because it only acts when a confirmed swing level breaks.

Q: Which chart does it need?

A: A Gold (XAUUSD, any suffix) chart on H1. It refuses other instruments and timeframes.

Q: Does it use martingale or a grid?

A: No. One position at a time, with a stop on every trade.

Q: When does it do well?

A: In trending phases of gold. In long quiet or choppy phases the number of trades falls and results flatten, which the research note shows.
