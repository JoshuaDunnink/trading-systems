Ridgeline - research note

Where it stands

Ridgeline is a swing-level breakout system for gold on H1. It is built from our own measurements of how this kind of system behaves across instruments and market regimes. It is not yet on sale.

What the tests show

The strategy tester results use real broker ticks, a 150 ms order delay and a 10,000 start deposit. The three regime windows are 2023-01 to 2024-09, 2024-10 to 2025-09 and 2025-11 to 2026-10. - On BlackBull the system was profitable in each window (profit factor from 1.52 to 3.09, worst equity drawdown 3.3 %). - On a second broker, VT Markets, it was profitable in each window as well (profit factor from 1.84 to 3.62). That broker's tick history is only partly real (70 %), so its numbers carry less weight. - On Pepperstone two of the three windows read positive (profit factor 2.80 and 1.72); the third did not finish. - The headline on the product page is the weaker broker, BlackBull, over the whole period.

What was learned

  • Stop, target and trail changes moved results only slightly: the geometry sits on a broad plateau, which is a sign of structure and not a fitted peak. Eight of nine target and trail combinations passed our rule.
  • Sizing the stop to gold's own volatility worked on gold. The same step did not hold up on US30, which we left out.
  • Silver and other instruments earned in some regimes and lost in others. A simple trend-state filter did not fix that, so they are not part of this product.
  • Edge per trade is small (a fraction of the risked amount), so spread and costs matter. Trade counts are low and differ by broker.
