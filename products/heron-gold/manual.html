Heron Gold - manual

1. Install and start (five minutes)

  1. Copy HERON_GOLD.ex5 into MQL5\Experts, restart the terminal or refresh the Navigator.
  2. Open a gold (XAUUSD) chart. M15 is the tested chart.
  3. Drag the EA onto the chart, tick Allow Algo Trading, keep the defaults and press OK.
  4. A status panel appears at the top left. It lists each part of the EA and shows whether it is watching, holding an order or in a trade.
  5. Run it on a demo account first: the tester results are not a forecast and the live fill path has not been tested.

2. Settings: start at the top

  • Lot size per order: 0.01 by default. This is the only setting you should need. Each order uses this size, and several orders can be open together.
  • Show the status panel: on by default.
  • All other inputs are research settings. Leave them at their defaults.

3. What it does, in order

Two parts share the account, as in Wren Gold. The swing part rests a stop order just beyond a confirmed swing high or low on the 15-minute chart; when it fills, it locks a small profit and trails the stop. The scalper rests stop orders just inside the hourly swing highs and lows with a stop of a few dollars and a tight trailing stop. A calm-market rule sits on top: while gold's average daily range of the last 14 closed days is below $80, no new orders are placed and resting orders are removed. Every position has a stop loss from the moment it opens.

4. How much money do I need? (minimum balance)

  • Margin: at gold near $4,500, 0.01 lot needs about $9 of margin at 1:500 and about $150 at 1:30.
  • Room for the stops: at 0.01 lot the largest single loss in the research run was about $16, and up to 2 positions were open at the same time.
  • What the research did: the claim run starts from a $1,000 deposit, where a 0.01 lot stop risks about 1 % of the balance. The same trades from a $100 start show a much larger drawdown percentage (backtest, defaults with fixed 0.01 lot, BlackBull, 2025-11-01..2026-10-30: 39.2 % at $100 against 8.8 % at $1,000), because the same dollars are a bigger share of a smaller balance. In older, calmer markets replayed at today's gold price, a $100 account was finished in some periods. A $100 account therefore has no room if gold turns calm; a larger balance gives more.

5. When it will not trade

  • When the market is closed or the spread is above the limit.
  • Around the US jobs report: the swing part removes its orders for a few hours on those days. The list of report dates inside the EA runs to December 2026 and needs updating after that.
  • While gold is calm (average daily range of the last 14 closed days below $80): the status panel shows the waiting parts.
  • On a broker whose server clock differs from the one it was tested on, session timings shift. Test on a demo account with your broker.

6. Known limits

  • It earns mainly in busy gold markets. In calm markets it earns little.
  • A few large winners make a share of the profit. The other trades add more slowly.
  • The first weeks can sit below the starting balance.
  • It can go for weeks without a trade when gold is calm.
  • Which side of the calm rule a given day falls on can differ from broker to broker, so a large trade can be taken on one broker and skipped on another.
  • Backtest results only: a demo forward test began on 8 November 2026 and there is no live record yet.
  • Past results do not predict future results.
