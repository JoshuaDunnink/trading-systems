He stands still when the water is calm: the same orders as Wren, but he places none while gold's daily range is small.

What it does. Gold breakout Expert Advisor with two parts on one account and a calm-market rule. The first part is a swing-breakout part that rests a stop order just beyond a confirmed 15-minute swing high or low, locks a small profit once the move starts and trails it. The second part is a fixed-dollar scalper that rests stop orders just inside the hourly swing highs and lows, with a stop of a few dollars and a tight trailing stop. The rule: while gold's average daily range of the last 14 closed days is below $80, Heron places no new orders and removes the ones that are resting. Every position has a stop loss from the moment it opens. No martingale, no grid, no averaging.

Instrument and chart. Gold (XAUUSD, any broker suffix). Tested on the M15 chart. The orders are placed by price level, so the chart period does not change the trades.

How it behaves. In a busy gold market Heron trades like Wren Gold, a little less often. In a calm market it stands aside, so it can go for weeks without a trade. The rule keeps it out of the calm periods in which the same parts lost money in older tests; it also means it can miss a large move that starts after a calm spell, and whether one trade falls on the right side of the rule can differ from broker to broker. A few large winners make a big share of the profit: the research note shows the same run without its biggest winners.

Settings. One setting matters: the lot size per order, 0.01 by default. The research inputs are not meant to be changed.

Please note. Results shown in the research note are strategy-tester results with stated limits. A forward test on a demo account began on 8 November 2026; there is no live record yet. Run it on a demo account first.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Do I need a .set file?

A: No. The defaults are the tested settings. Leave the other inputs alone.

Q: What is the minimum balance?

A: The manual explains it. In short: 0.01 lot needs about $9 of margin at 1:500 and about $150 at 1:30 with gold near $4,500, and the largest single loss in the research run was about $16 at that size, so a small account has little room.

Q: Which chart does it need?

A: A gold chart (XAUUSD). It was tested on M15.

Q: Does it use martingale or a grid?

A: No. Every position has its own stop loss, and the lot size is fixed.
