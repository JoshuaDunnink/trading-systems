Quick and low to the ground. She waits just inside yesterday's highs and lows and takes the short run when gold breaks through.

What it does. Gold scalping Expert Advisor. It tracks daily highs and lows from the last two weeks that have not been broken and that stand out on the hourly chart. When price comes within a few dollars of one, it places pending stop orders just inside the level, each with its own small fixed stop and a larger target. Once a trade is in profit its stop moves up behind price. Pending orders are cancelled on Fridays at 22:00 server time. No martingale, no grid, no averaging; every position has a hard stop.

Instrument and chart. Gold (XAUUSD, any broker suffix). Tested on the H1 chart.

How it behaves. Pipit places orders when gold approaches a recent high or low, and most trades last a few seconds to a few minutes. Several orders can fill together when a level breaks. In quiet markets it makes little; in fast gold it trades often. Because trades are so short, real fills in fast markets can differ from the tester.

Please note. Results in the research note are strategy-tester results with stated limits. There is no live record yet. Run it on a demo account first.
