Plover Gold - manual

1. Install and start (five minutes)

  1. Copy PLOVER_GOLD.ex5 into MQL5\Experts, restart the terminal or refresh the Navigator.
  2. Open a gold (XAUUSD) chart on M1.
  3. Drag the EA onto the chart, tick Allow Algo Trading, keep the defaults and press OK.
  4. Run it on a demo account first: tester results are not a forecast and the live fill path has not been tested.

2. Settings: start at the top

  • Lot size: lot 0.01 per $1,000 of balance (capped at 3 % risk per trade). This is the setting to review first.
  • All other inputs are research settings. Leave them at their defaults.

3. What it does, in order

  1. Signal. Each new one-minute bar it checks whether the 21-period RSI has just climbed back out of below 25 (a buy setup) or dropped back from above 72 (a sell setup).
  2. Trend side. A buy needs price above the hourly 50-period average. A sell needs price below it.
  3. Busy gold only. The average hourly range of the last 14 hours must be above a floor, and the spread must be small.
  4. Sizing. The lot is 0.01 per $1,000 of balance, capped so the stop risks at most 3 % of the balance.
  5. Exit. A stop at four times and a target at two times the one-minute range, set when the order is sent. After two losing trades in one day it stops until the next day.

4. How much money do I need? (minimum balance)

  • The research run started from $10,000. The largest single loss in that run was about $293.
  • At 1:30 leverage 0.01 lot needs about $150 of margin with gold near $4,500; a small account has little room for a losing run.

5. When it will not trade

  • On Fridays, and before 09:00 or after midnight server time.
  • When gold's hourly range is below the floor or the spread is wide.
  • When the market is closed or the spread is above the limit.
  • On a broker whose server clock differs from the tested one, session timings shift. Test on a demo account with your broker.

6. Known limits

  • It earns mainly in busy gold markets.
  • Several losses in a row are normal for a snap-back system; the daily two-loss stop limits a bad day.
  • Backtest results only; there is no live record yet.
  • Past results do not predict future results.
