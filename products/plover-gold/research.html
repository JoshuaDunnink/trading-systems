All figures: Backtest, MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, real ticks, $10,000 deposit, lot 0.01 per $1,000 of balance (capped at 3 % risk per trade), 150 ms delay, leverage 1:500, defaults (no .set), version 1.00. Window 2025-11-11..2026-11-11.

What it is

Plover Gold is a one-minute snap-back scalper. When the 21-period RSI climbs back out of an extreme zone (below 25 or above 72) it trades the move back toward the middle, with a stop and a target sized from gold's current one-minute range. It trades only when gold's hourly range is large enough, buys only while price is above its hourly 50-period average, sells only while price is below it, and stops for the day after two losing trades.

What the curve looks like

  • Concentration. Without its 7 biggest winners (the top 5 % of 152 trades) the BlackBull run makes $4,202 of its $5,466. The page shows both curves in dollars, not on a log scale.
  • One kind of market. The window is the busy gold market since November 2025. Replays of older, calmer gold at today's price were flat or lost money, so do not expect it to profit in every market.
