Quick and watchful. He darts in when gold has stretched too far, too fast, and takes the small snap back toward the middle.

What it does. Gold scalping Expert Advisor for the one-minute chart. It waits for the 21-period RSI to climb back out of an extreme zone and trades the snap back toward the middle. The stop is four times and the target two times gold's average one-minute range, so both widen and narrow with the market. It trades only when gold's hourly range is large enough to move, only buys while price is above its hourly 50-period average and only sells while price is below it. One position at a time, a hard stop on every trade, no martingale, no grid, no averaging.

Instrument and chart. Gold (XAUUSD, any broker suffix). Tested on the M1 chart.

How it behaves. Plover trades a few times a week in a busy gold market and is usually done within minutes. It stands aside when gold is quiet, on Fridays and during the first and last hours of the day. After two losing trades in a day it stops until the next day.

Please note. Results in the research note are strategy-tester results with stated limits. There is no live record yet. Run it on a demo account first.
