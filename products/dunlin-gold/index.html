A shorebird that works the tideline. He trades gold when the market moves in orderly swings with a lean to one side, and stands back when it turns rough.

What it does. Gold Expert Advisor driven by a small neural network. Every 15 minutes the network reads the last two and a half days of candles and decides whether gold is in an orderly, range-like stretch with a lean up or down. If it is, the EA opens a trading window on that side and takes up to three positions. Every position has a stop loss from the moment it opens. The stop moves to entry after a small favourable move, then locks profit and trails. A position that has not recovered after 30 minutes and has given back half of the move that opened the window is closed. After a full stop loss the EA takes no more trades on that side that day.

A rough-phase switch. The EA also follows every trade it would have taken as a virtual position. While more than a fifth of the virtual trades of the last four weeks ended in a loss, it takes no new trades until the picture improves.

Instrument and chart. Gold (XAUUSD, any broker suffix), any chart period. A hedging account is required. The server clock of the broker should be EET with US daylight saving; the manual lists brokers and the setting to change otherwise.

How it behaves. About two trades a day on average, many ending within an hour. It earns in orderly gold markets. In older, rougher markets replayed at today's gold price the research tests lost money in some periods and were flat in others. It was not built to catch every big move.

Settings. One setting matters: lot per $1,000 of balance, 0.01 by default. Everything else is at its tested value.

Please note. Results shown in the research note are strategy-tester results with stated limits. There is no live record yet. Run it on a demo account first.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Do I need a .set file?

A: No. The defaults are the tested settings.

Q: What is the minimum balance?

A: The manual explains it. In short: below $1,000 every trade is the 0.01 minimum lot, so a stop takes a larger share of a small balance. The research suggests $300 at 1:500 leverage or $400 at 1:100 as a practical minimum.

Q: Does it need an internet connection or a DLL?

A: No. The neural network is compiled into the EA and runs inside MetaTrader 5.

Q: Does it use martingale or a grid?

A: No. Every position has its own stop loss and the lot does not grow after a loss.

Q: Which account type?

A: A hedging account, because it can hold up to three positions on gold at once.
