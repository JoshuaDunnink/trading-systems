Dunlin Gold - manual

1. Install and start (five minutes)

  1. Copy DUNLIN_GOLD.ex5 into MQL5\Experts, restart the terminal or refresh the Navigator. The neural network is inside the file; no other file is needed.
  2. Open a gold (XAUUSD) chart on a hedging account. Any chart period works; the EA reads 15-minute candles.
  3. Drag the EA onto the chart, tick Allow Algo Trading, pick a Risk profile (Default if unsure, Safe for a small account) and press OK.
  4. The Experts tab prints a line starting "NOVA_LIVE v0.31" if it started correctly. If the account is netting, the EA refuses to start and says why.
  5. Run it on a demo account first: the tester results are not a forecast and the live fill path has not been tested.

2. Settings: start at the top

Two inputs sit under Start here; everything below them is advanced and stays at its default. - Risk profile: Safe, Default (preset), Risky or Custom. - Lot per $1,000 of balance: 0.01 by default. Below $1,000 each trade is the broker minimum lot (0.01).

If the balance is below the amount in the last column it prints a warning, and still trades the profile you chose.

3. What it does, in order

  1. Every 15 minutes the neural network reads the last two and a half days of candles.
  2. If it finds an orderly, range-like stretch with a lean up or down, a trading window opens on that side and up to three positions are taken.
  3. Every position has a stop loss. After a small favourable move the stop moves to entry; after a larger one profit is locked and trailed.
  4. A position still under water after 30 minutes that has given back half of the opening move is closed.
  5. After a full stop loss, no new trade on that side for the rest of the day.
  6. If more than a fifth of the virtual trades of the last four weeks were losses, no new trades are taken until that improves.

4. How much money do I need? (minimum balance)

  • Trading hours: entries are made between 03:00 and 15:45 server time.
  • Size of one full stop at $1,000: Safe about 3 % of the balance, Default about 6 %, Risky about 12 %.
  • Balance per profile (5,000 simulated 12-month paths per broker, built from 5-day blocks of the backtest's daily results; backtest, Strategy Tester, real ticks, BlackBull, IC Markets and VT Markets, 2025-09-01..2026-11-18):

  • Small accounts: below $1,000 every trade is the 0.01 minimum lot, so a stop takes a larger share of the balance. Safe is the profile for small accounts.
  • These figures cover one market period (15 months). Older, rougher gold markets replayed at today's price had deeper losing stretches, so treat them as a floor, not a promise.

5. Requirements

  • Hedging account, gold symbol with any suffix, one chart per account.
  • Broker server clock EET with US daylight saving (BlackBull, IC Markets EU, VT Markets, Vantage and Fusion all are). On another clock the entry hours must be shifted.
  • Raw-spread account, algorithmic trading on.

6. Known limits

  • It earns in orderly gold markets. In rough stretches the rough-phase switch stops new trades, and older rough markets replayed at today's price still produced losing periods.
  • Volatility-shock days, with stops hit within minutes, are its weak spot.
  • Backtest results only; there is no live record yet.
