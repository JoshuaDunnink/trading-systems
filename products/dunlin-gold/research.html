Dunlin Gold - research note (every number names its ARM, FEED and WINDOW)

All figures: MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, real ticks, $1,000 deposit, default lot (0.01 per $1,000 of balance), 150 ms delay, leverage 1:500, version 0.23 defaults (no .set), window 2025-09-01..2026-11-14. Arm: "Defaults", version 0.23. The IC Markets and VT Markets rows replay the same version's trade lists at a fixed 0.01 lot from $1,000.

What it is

Gold neural-network window trader. A small neural network reads the last two and a half days of 15-minute candles and decides whether gold is in an orderly, range-like stretch with a lean up or down; if so it opens a trading window and takes up to three positions on that side. Every position has a stop loss from the moment it opens. Profit is protected in stages: the stop moves to entry after a small favourable move, then locks profit and trails. No martingale, no grid, no averaging down.

How the network was made

It was trained on 2024-12 to 2025-06-30 gold and on many copies of that market: the same days with the clock shifted, older gold history (2013 onward) rescaled to today's price, time-stretched and amplitude-matched, and about forty synthetic paths built from real days, each flipped upside-down with probability one half. Training stops at 2025-06-30; everything after is data the network never saw.

What the curve looks like

  • Without its biggest winners it still earns. Without the biggest winners (5 % of trades) (35 trades) the BlackBull run makes $725 of its $1,027. The page shows both curves in dollars.
  • Most months earn. 14 of 15 calendar months are positive on BlackBull.
  • It was tested in one kind of market. The window is the orderly-to-busy gold market of 2025-26. In older gold markets replayed at today's price it lost money in some stretches; the rough-phase switch cut those losses by roughly a quarter in the research tests but did not remove them.

Reading it honestly

  • The entry threshold of the network was chosen on this same window among seven values; the network itself never saw the window. The forward record is the clean test.
  • The tester cannot show slippage on stops in fast markets.
  • After a terminal restart the rough-phase switch is open until five virtual trades have resolved again.
  • There is no live record yet. Nothing here is a live result.
