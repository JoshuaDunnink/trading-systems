Trebuchet - user manual (draft for the research build 0.24)

1. What it does

Trebuchet trades gold (XAUUSD) breakouts of a price channel with seven exit legs per breakout. See the listing for the full description. Nothing here is financial advice. Trading leveraged products carries a high risk of loss. Past results, backtested or live, do not predict future results.

2. Installing

Attach the EA to any XAUUSD chart on a hedging account and enable algorithmic trading. It needs no set file. Keep the terminal running (VPS or always-on PC): the break-even and the protection locks run inside the EA.

3. Modes

Mode What changes
Default near-miss protection on (burst lock and near-target lock), spread-aware break-even
Selective as Default, and no new orders while gold's recent daily range is in the busiest 40 percent of its own past year
Maximum protection as Default, and a trailing stop over the last stretch to the target

4. Inputs that matter

Input Default Meaning
Lot per order 0.01 fixed lot for every leg (balance-based sizing in preparation)
Maximum spread 300 points a new order waits while the spread is wider
Burst lock 40 % within 3 min, keep 30 % fast spikes toward the target
Near-target lock at 75 %, keep 20 % trades close to their target
Busy-market filter off (on in Selective) stand aside in busy markets
Trailing stop off (on in Maximum protection) from 90 % of the way, 15 % of the target behind the price
