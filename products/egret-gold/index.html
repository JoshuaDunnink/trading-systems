An egret stands still in the shallows and strikes only when the fish comes close. Egret Gold waits for a short dip inside an established gold trend and takes it.

What it does. Gold Expert Advisor with five small strategies that share one idea: buy a shallow dip while the trend points up, sell a short rally while it points down. Each strategy reads a fast RSI dip on a short chart period, an EMA trend on a longer one and a minimum of market activity in dollars, and each adds its own filter (an 8-hour trend check, the 200-period average, a guard against sudden spikes, a minimum of short-term movement). A trade closes at a modest target sized to the pullback it bought. A trade that has not worked after three hours is closed at break-even, after four hours at a small loss. Every trade has a hard stop loss from the moment it opens.

Five strategies, one account. Each strategy holds at most one position per side and waits at least four hours between its own entries. Strategies may be open at the same time, at most three positions in total. A safety setting refuses a new trade when the stop losses of all open positions together would risk more than 20 % of the balance.

How it behaves. About 32 trades a month in the busy gold market of 2025-26, most of them closed within the hour. In the quieter gold market of 2023-24 it traded far less, three to four times a month, with a small profit. It earns most when gold trends with regular pullbacks.

Settings. One setting matters: the risk level (Low, Medium, High or a fixed lot). Low is the default and was used for every figure shown. Five switches turn single strategies off. Everything else is at its tested value; no .set file is needed.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Do I need a .set file?

A: No. Attach it to a gold chart, any period, and it runs its tested settings.

Q: What is the minimum balance?

A: $1,000 was the tested deposit. Below $2,000 the Low risk level trades the broker's minimum lot, 0.01.

Q: Does it use martingale or a grid?

A: No. Every position has its own stop loss, the lot does not grow after a loss, and it never adds to a losing position.

Q: Why can several positions be open at once?

A: Each of the five strategies may hold one position per side. The 20 % stop-risk setting limits how many can be open together.

Q: Which account type?

A: A hedging account, because several strategies may hold positions on gold at the same time.
