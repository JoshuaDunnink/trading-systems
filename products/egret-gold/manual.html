Egret Gold - user manual

Installation

  1. Copy EGRET_GOLD.ex5 into MQL5/Experts of your MetaTrader 5 data folder (or install it from the Market).
  2. Open a gold chart (XAUUSD with any broker suffix), any chart period.
  3. Drag the EA onto the chart and allow algorithmic trading. No .set file is needed.

The account must allow hedging, because several strategies may hold positions on gold at once.

Inputs

1. Start here

Input Default Meaning
Risk level Low Low 0.005 lot, Medium 0.01 lot, High 0.02 lot per $1,000 of balance per strategy, or a fixed lot. Below the broker minimum the EA trades the minimum (0.01).
Lot when Risk = Fixed lot 0.01 Used only with the fixed-lot risk level.
Size as if balance were at most 0 (off) Caps the balance used for sizing.
Skip entries above this spread 1.5 USD No new trade while the spread is wider.

2. Strategies

Five switches: A and B (2-minute RSI dip with a 5- or 15-minute trend) and E (2-minute dip above the 200-period average) are on by default; C (34-minute drop inside a 24-hour trend) and D (1-minute dip in a strong 5-minute trend) are off by default.

3. Safety

Input Default Meaning
Magic number 88990 Identifies the EA's own positions.
Order comment prefix EGRET
Max open positions per direction 1 At most this many buys (and this many sells) open at once across all strategies. 0 turns it off.
Max total stop risk 0 (off) Refuses a new trade when the stop losses of all open positions plus the new one would exceed this share of the balance.

Risk levels

The figures on the product page use Low. Medium doubles every lot once the balance is above $1,000 and High doubles it again; dips grow in proportion. Use Medium or High only if you accept deeper dips.

How trades close

  • Target: about a quarter of the pullback the entry bought, at least $2.50 per ounce.
  • After three hours a trade closes as soon as it is back at break-even.
  • After four hours a trade closes as soon as its loss is within $18.40 per ounce.
  • Hard stop loss on every trade: $75 per ounce.
  • By default at most one buy and one sell open at once.

Troubleshooting

  • No trades for days: normal in quiet gold markets; the EA needs a trend with pullbacks and enough activity.
  • "Hedging account required": switch to a hedging account type with your broker.
