He rides the swing of the NASDAQ's US session momentum and is always flat by the close.

What it does. Momentum oscillator on the NASDAQ 100 during the US cash session (09:30 to 16:00 New York). A position opens when the oscillator reaches an extreme, with a hard stop at 2.5 % of price, and is closed by the opposite extreme or at the end of the session. No position is held overnight.

Instrument and chart. NASDAQ 100 (NAS100 / US100), M10 chart. Holds a trade about 6 hours (median), flat before the New York close, fixed 2.5 % hard stop, trades only the US session.

Settings. Pendulum has one main input, the risk level. It runs one position at a time with a stop on every trade, without martingale, grid or averaging.

Please note. Results shown in the research note are strategy-tester results with stated limits. Run on a demo account first.

Frequently asked questions

Q: Do I need a .set file?

A: No. The defaults are the validated settings; you choose a risk level (Low, Medium or High).

Q: What is the minimum balance?

A: About $2,000. Below that the broker's minimum lot becomes the risk instead of the setting.

Q: Which chart does it need?

A: A NASDAQ 100 (NAS100 / US100) chart on M10. It refuses other instruments.

Q: Does it use martingale or a grid?

A: No. One position at a time, with a stop on every trade.
