Pendulum - research note (every number names its ARM, FEED and WINDOW)

All figures: MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, $10,000 deposit, 150 ms execution delay, Medium risk, compounding, defaults (arm "Defaults (Medium)", version 1.00), window 2022-11-01..2026-10-27.

What it is

Momentum oscillator on the NASDAQ 100 during the US cash session (09:30 to 16:00 New York). A position opens when the oscillator reaches an extreme, with a hard stop at 2.5 % of price, and is closed by the opposite extreme or at the end of the session. No position is held overnight.

Reading it honestly

  • The largest single loss in the test was about 9 % of the balance at the Medium setting (the hard stop is wide); Low halves that.
  • Needs the broker clock to be set correctly against New York time (see the manual).
  • About one trade in two wins; index CFD costs and session hours differ per broker.
  • Two market windows (Nov 2022 to Oct 2024 and Nov 2024 to Oct 2026) were used to choose the settings; the design was fixed at the values above, not tuned to this table.
  • Nothing here is a live record.
