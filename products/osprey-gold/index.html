Patient, then sudden. He watches the lines gold keeps drawing and dives the moment a break looks overdone.

What it does. Gold reversal Expert Advisor for the 15-minute chart. It draws trendlines through recent swing highs and swing lows, and when price closes through a line it trades the other way, on the idea that most such breaks run out of steam. Every trade has a hard stop of $50 per ounce from the first tick; once it is $25 in profit a trailing stop follows $10 behind. Up to three positions at a time. No martingale, no grid, no averaging.

Instrument and chart. Gold (XAUUSD, any broker suffix). Tested on the M15 chart.

How it behaves. Osprey trades several times a week. Winners are usually closed by the trailing stop within hours; a losing trade is closed at its fixed stop. A single losing run can be several trades long, so size the account for it.

Please note. Results in the research note are strategy-tester results with stated limits. There is no live record yet. Run it on a demo account first.
