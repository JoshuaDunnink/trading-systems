Osprey Gold - research note (every number names its ARM, FEED and WINDOW)

All figures: Backtest, MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester, real ticks, $10,000 deposit, fixed 0.02 lot, 150 ms delay, leverage 1:500, defaults (no .set), version 1.00. Window 2025-11-11..2026-11-11.

What it is

Osprey Gold is a 15-minute reversal system. It draws falling and rising trendlines through recent swing highs and lows and takes the opposite side the moment price closes through one, with a hard stop on every trade and a trailing stop that starts once the trade is in profit.

What the curve looks like

  • Concentration. Without its 10 biggest winners (the top 5 % of 202 trades) the VT Markets run makes $2,603 of its $3,682. The page shows both curves in dollars, not on a log scale.
  • One kind of market. The window is the busy gold market since November 2025. Replays of older, calmer gold at today's price were flat or lost money, so do not expect it to profit in every market.

Reading it honestly
