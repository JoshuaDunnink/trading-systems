Osprey Gold - manual

1. Install and start (five minutes)

  1. Copy OSPREY_GOLD.ex5 into MQL5\Experts, restart the terminal or refresh the Navigator.
  2. Open a gold (XAUUSD) chart on M15.
  3. Drag the EA onto the chart, tick Allow Algo Trading, keep the defaults and press OK.
  4. Run it on a demo account first: tester results are not a forecast and the live fill path has not been tested.

2. Settings: start at the top

  • Lot size: fixed 0.02 lot. This is the setting to review first.
  • All other inputs are research settings. Leave them at their defaults.

3. What it does, in order

  1. Lines. From swing highs and swing lows on the 15-minute chart it draws falling lines through consecutive highs and rising lines through consecutive lows.
  2. Break. When a bar closes through a line it counts as a break, and the line is used once.
  3. Reversal. An upward break gives a sell, a downward break gives a buy.
  4. Filters. No trades between 01:00 and 10:00 server time, a minimum trend-strength reading, and a higher-timeframe direction check.
  5. Exit. A $50 stop from the first tick. Once the trade is $25 in profit, a trailing stop follows $10 behind price. Account guards stop it for the day after a 4 % loss and for good after an 8 % loss.

4. How much money do I need? (minimum balance)

  • The research run started from $10,000. The largest single loss in that run was about $110.
  • At 1:30 leverage 0.01 lot needs about $150 of margin with gold near $4,500; a small account has little room for a losing run.

5. When it will not trade

  • Between 01:00 and 10:00 server time.
  • After the daily 4 % loss guard or the 8 % account guard has triggered.
  • When the market is closed or the spread is above the limit.
  • On a broker whose server clock differs from the tested one, session timings shift. Test on a demo account with your broker.

6. Known limits

  • A single losing run can be several trades long.
  • The 8 % account guard assumes a $10,000 account; on a smaller account lower the lot size instead.
  • Backtest results only; there is no live record yet.
  • Past results do not predict future results.
