Up before the market, Daybreak chalks yesterday's high and low on the wall and waits at the line. When price crosses it, he goes with the first burst and is out again in seconds - one level, one trade, and a quiet day if nothing breaks.

Daybreak Gold is a breakout system for gold. Every trading day it sets two prices from the previous day's high and low. A break through either level opens one position - never more than one at a time - with a stop loss and a take profit measured from the level itself, so every trade carries the same risk distance regardless of the fill. Once a position is far enough in profit, a trailing stop takes over and gives back only a fixed amount from the best price reached.

The strategy skips new entries on the day of a US employment report, a Federal Reserve rate decision, and the thinly-traded days around the year-end close. It also holds new entries on a day whose prior 24 hours moved too little, unless the break reaches into price the market has not traded in five days.

The results below come from gold in 2025 and 2026, a high-volatility period; the same rules run on gold from 2018 to 2024 were flat to slightly negative. This is a system for a moving gold market, not an all-weather one.

No grid. No martingale. No averaging into a losing position. One risk setting (Risk level: Low, Medium or High) controls position size as a percent of balance per stop; every other input is open for review.

Frequently asked questions

Does it hedge, martingale or grid? No. One position at a time, always with a stop loss.

What symbol and timeframe? Gold only. Any chart timeframe works - the strategy reads the daily bar and a fixed M15 clock internally regardless of the chart it is attached to.

What controls risk? The Risk level input (Low / Medium / High), or a custom risk percent / fixed lot under Risk level = Custom.

Does it work on a prop-firm account? Account type includes Prop firm 2-step and Prop firm 1-step presets that add a daily-loss and maximum-loss guard sized to typical challenge rules; a Prop-firm rules section lets you enter your own firm's exact numbers.

Why doesn't it trade every day? Two levels a day, at most one trade per level, and several days are held entirely (thin prior-day range, a scheduled calendar event). This is by design.
