Risk and privacy

Risk

Trading leveraged products carries a high risk of loss. Every performance figure on this site is a backtest unless it is marked as the live signal. Past results, backtested or live, do not predict future results. Nothing here is financial advice.

Backtests are run in the MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester on real broker ticks. They include spread, commission and swap as the broker recorded them, but they cannot include every live effect: slippage, requotes, latency and a different broker's prices all change results. Test every Expert Advisor on a demo account with your own broker before trading real money, and only trade money you can afford to lose.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, as its documentation states. Purchases, licences and payments are handled by mql5.com under its own terms.
