Gold market conditions, month by month

Some months reward breakouts. Others take them back. This page measures, for every month since November 2022, how gold actually traded: how far it moved, how much of that movement was trend rather than back-and-forth, and how often a break of an important price level kept going. It describes the market. It contains no trading results.

How it is measured

  • Data. Every tick of BlackBull Markets gold (XAUUSD) price history in MetaTrader 5, on the broker's server clock.
  • Important levels. The previous day's high and low, and the nearest swing high and swing low on the 1-hour and 4-hour charts that price has not yet broken. These are the levels where many traders keep their stop orders.
  • Follow-through. At the exact tick a level breaks, we simulate a trade in the direction of the break, paying the spread and a commission of $0.06 per ounce. The break counts as followed through when price runs $2 further before it pulls back $1. Only breaks that settle within two hours are counted.
  • Chance level. If price moved at random, about 29 % of breaks would follow through, because the target is twice as far as the pullback and the spread is paid at entry.
  • Character of the month.
    • Breakouts followed through: 50 % or more of the breaks kept going.
    • Breakouts mostly reversed: 30 % or less kept going; breaks were usually taken back.
    • Mixed: in between.
    • In the chart, green bars are months where breakouts followed through, orange bars months where they mostly reversed, grey bars mixed months; the dashed line is the chance level.
  • Other columns. Average daily range and median hourly range in dollars; trend days = share of days whose net move was at least 60 % of the day's range; persistence = how strongly 15-minute moves carried on over four hours (above 1 trending, below 1 back-and-forth).

What the record shows

From November 2025 to August 2026, eight of ten months had breakouts that followed through: gold's large, news-driven swings carried on after important levels broke. Before that, most months sat close to the chance level. Some months, such as August 2023, September 2024 and September 2026, took most breaks back.

Our gold systems mostly earn the most in months when gold moves far and breaks keep going, and less in months when breaks are taken back. How strongly a system depends on the breakout character differs: for some, the size of gold's moves explains most of the difference between months; for others, whether breaks follow through matters as well.
